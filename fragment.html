What it does

The jobs it handles every day

These are the jobs it runs inside my own company. Yours gets the ones that fit how you work, and we add more as you come to trust it.

Manages your email

Sorts the noise from the money and flags anything with a date in it. A request with a deadline becomes an alert on your phone the same hour, not a surprise next week.

Keeps follow-ups going

A steady number of follow-ups every day, on the schedule you set, to the prospects you choose. It stops the moment someone replies, so nobody gets a form letter after they already wrote back.

Drafts your quotes and estimates

When a supplier's price lands, it builds your customer quote in your own format with your usual markup and puts it in Drafts, ready for you to send the same day.

Protects your customer relationships

It is given your customer list and checks it before every automated send, so an existing account is not treated like a stranger. Existing accounts stay out of automated lists unless you say otherwise.

Does the recurring paperwork

Expense reports, weekly activity summaries, the Monday numbers for the team, the reminder that the report is due on the 1st. Drafted on time from the real records.

Remembers what you tell it

Every promise, price, visit and "never call that supplier again" is kept in its notes on your server. Ask about a customer and get the answer, not a search.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers

What is an AI assistant, in plain terms?

A program that can read, write and keep notes, connected to your email, calendar and files so it can act on them. The difference between this and the chatbots you've seen is that this one is set up around your business, keeps its notes, and has a person managing it.

Which AI is it?

Claude, made by Anthropic, running on a server rented in your name with a Claude subscription in your name. The setting that allows conversations to be used for training is turned off on day one.

Can it see my email?

Yes, that is the job. It reads your mail on your server, in your account. I have administrator access to that server so I can fix things, and I look only when you've asked me to. I will never ask you for a password.

Will it send things without asking?

Not until you say so. Every job starts in approval mode. When you're comfortable, you switch routine jobs to run on their own and keep approval on for anything that goes to a customer.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can send a text message, you can use it. There is nothing to install on your computer and nothing to configure. I handle all of that.

What does it connect to?

Whatever you run the business on, as long as it offers a proper way to connect. Outlook or Gmail, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, your calendar, your CRM, QuickBooks, your files, your chat app. There is no cap on how many.

What if I don't like it?

Care is month to month. You own the server and the accounts, so if you stop, nothing disappears and nothing is held hostage.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

It will, especially in the first weeks. You tell me, I find out why, and I write the rule that stops it from happening again.

Where are you?

Middle Tennessee. Everything on this page is done remotely, so where you are doesn't matter.
