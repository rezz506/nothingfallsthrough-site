What it does

The jobs it runs every day

These run inside my company right now. Yours gets the ones that fit how you work, and we add more as you trust it.

Watches the inbox

Sorts the noise from the money and flags anything with a date in it. A request with a deadline is an alert on your phone the same hour.

Keeps follow-ups going

A steady number of touches every day on the schedule you set. It stops the moment someone replies, so nobody gets a canned follow-up after they wrote back.

Turns vendor quotes into customer quotes

When a supplier's price lands, it builds your quote in your format with your markup and puts it in Drafts, ready to send the same day.

Protects your relationships

It knows who your customers are and never treats one like a stranger. Existing accounts stay out of every automated list unless you release them.

Does the recurring paperwork

Expense reports, weekly activity summaries, the Monday numbers for the team. Drafted on time from the real records, without being asked twice.

Remembers everything

Every promise, markup, plant visit and "never contact that supplier again" lives in its memory on your server. Ask about any account and get the answer, not a search.

I build it, I watch it, and when it gets something wrong I write the rule so it never happens again. That loop is the product.

Your own server

A small private server you own, on your own Claude subscription, behind your own private network. Your records never pass through my systems. If we part ways, you keep everything.

Approval is a setting

Every new job starts with you approving each action. When you trust it, you switch approval off for that job and it runs on its own. You decide how fast.

Who's behind it

I didn't build this to sell it. I built it because I was the bottleneck in my own company.

I sell presses and automation for my family's industrial equipment company. Small team, a sales territory, and for years the job was a hundred open loops and a phone that never stopped. So I put an assistant on a server, connected it to everything, and taught it the rules of the business one miss at a time.

Every rule it follows today came from something that slipped once. A quote that sat. A follow-up that went to someone who had already replied. A deadline I found too late. Each one became a check it runs on every job, every time.

That is what you're paying for. Not the software, which is Claude and a server. The rules, and someone who keeps writing them.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers

Which AI is it?

Claude, made by Anthropic, running on a server you own with a subscription in your name. The setting that lets conversations train their models is turned off on day one.

Can it see my email?

Yes, that is the job. It reads your mail on your server, in your account. I only see what you choose to show me, and I will never ask you for a password.

Will it send things without asking?

Not until you say so. Every job starts in approval mode. Most owners switch the routine jobs to run on their own within a few weeks and keep approval on for anything that goes to a customer.

What does it connect to?

Whatever you run the business on. Microsoft 365 or Google, your calendar, your CRM, your files, your chat app, your accounting. There is no limit on how many.

What if I don't like it?

Care is month to month. You own the server and the accounts, so if you stop, nothing disappears and nothing is held hostage.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

It will, especially in the first weeks. You tell me, I find out why, and I write the rule that stops it from happening again.

Where are you?

Middle Tennessee. Everything on this page is done remotely, so where you are doesn't matter.
